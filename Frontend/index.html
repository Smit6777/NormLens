About NormLens

NormLens is an institutional artificial intelligence decision-support platform designed to streamline public and enterprise procurement in India. Developed for Smart India Hackathon (SIH) 2026 Problem Statement 26108, the system bridges the critical gap between complex technical procurement tender specifications and the authoritative catalogue of Indian Standards maintained by the Bureau of Indian Standards (BIS).

Why It Was Created

Public procurement officers frequently encounter ambiguous technical terminology or outdated standards references in tender documents, leading to compliance disputes, delivery rejections, and legal delays. NormLens automates standard discovery with 100% version accuracy.

How AI Assists Procurement

By utilizing dense semantic indexing, technical taxonomy extraction, and normative cross-reference mapping, our engine evaluates requirements against thousands of gazette standards in milliseconds while verifying active Quality Control Orders (QCO).
